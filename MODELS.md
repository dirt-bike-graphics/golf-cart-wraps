## Practical notes

Compare the front cowl, left side, right side and rear body separately. Match the roof canopy and seats to the wrap in the 3D preview. Roof information is included only when it appears in a recorded body description.

These are visual body references, without physical measurements, DPI specifications or production cut templates. A shared brand or model name does not establish panel interchangeability. The Precedent i2 reference year remains unconfirmed.
